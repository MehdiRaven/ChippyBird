# 🐦 About Chippy Bird

**Chippy Bird** is a challenging Android arcade game created by
**Raven Studio**.

Inspired by the classic tap-to-fly gameplay concept, Chippy Bird
takes the familiar experience and expands it with multiple visual
themes, unique music, special abilities, and challenging game modes.

The controls are simple.

The challenge is not.

Players can choose different environments and game modes, each offering
a different gameplay experience and requiring different levels of
timing, concentration, and reaction.

# 🎮 Five Game Modes

Chippy Bird features **five different game modes**, ranging from the
classic experience to highly challenging gameplay.

---

## 🟢 Classic Mode

The classic Chippy Bird experience.

This mode follows the familiar tap-to-fly gameplay style.

### Gameplay

- 🐦 Control the bird by tapping
- 🚧 Navigate through the pipes
- 🎯 Avoid obstacles
- 🏆 Survive and achieve a higher score

Simple controls.

Classic gameplay.

Endless challenge.

---

## ⭐ Power Mode

A special gameplay mode that introduces a temporary protective ability.

Throughout the level, players can collect **stars**.

When a star is collected:

### 🛡️ The bird becomes protected for 3 seconds.

During this time, the bird is safe from the pipes.

The ability creates opportunities for players to take risks and
navigate difficult situations while the protection is active.

### ⭐ Power System

- ⭐ Collect stars
- 🛡️ Activate temporary protection
- ⏱️ Protection lasts for 3 seconds
- 🚧 Pipes become harmless during the protection period
- 🎯 Use the ability strategically

---

# 🔥 Challenge Modes

The Challenge section contains **three different game modes** designed
to push the player's reactions and control to the limit.

---

## 🔴 Moving Pipes

The pipes are no longer static.

### 🚧 The obstacles move.

Players must constantly adapt their movement and timing to changing
pipe positions.

This mode requires much faster reactions and significantly more precise
control than the classic experience.

### The Challenge

- 🚧 Moving pipes
- 🧠 Constant adaptation
- 🎯 Precise timing
- ⚡ Fast reactions
- 🔥 High difficulty

---

## ⚡ Speed Mode

Speed Mode takes the classic gameplay and dramatically increases
the pace.

The gameplay is similar to Classic Mode, but the bird moves at
approximately **twice the normal speed**.

### ⚡ Almost 2× Speed

The increased speed means:

- ⚡ Less reaction time
- 🎯 More precise control required
- 🧠 Faster decision making
- 🔥 Much more intense gameplay

A familiar mode with a completely different difficulty.

---

## 🌑 Darkness Mode

One of the most challenging modes in Chippy Bird.

The environment becomes almost completely dark.

The player can only clearly see the bird, surrounded by a small
**halo of light**.

### 🌑 Limited Visibility

The obstacles remain hidden in the darkness until the bird gets close
to them.

This forces players to rely on:

- 👁️ Quick visual reactions
- 🧠 Concentration
- 🎯 Precise timing
- ⚡ Fast decision making

When an obstacle suddenly appears within the bird's limited visibility,
the player has only a short moment to react.

### 🌑 Darkness Challenge

> **You can't see what's coming.  
> You only see what's close.**
